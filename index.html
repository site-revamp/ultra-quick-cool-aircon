Common aircon problems

Not sure what your aircon needs?

Tell us what is happening. We can help you identify which service to enquire about.

Aircon not cold

Dirty coils or restricted airflow may affect cooling. A technician can inspect and advise whether a deeper clean is needed.

Water leaking

Blocked drainage or a dirty drain tray may cause water to drip indoors.

Musty smell

Moisture and dirt around the coil or blower may contribute to unpleasant odours.

Slow cooling

A dirty indoor coil or outdoor condenser can affect heat transfer and cooling performance.

Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

How much does general aircon servicing cost?

The published rate is $40 for one unit, $25 per unit for two, $20 per unit for three or more, and $15 per unit for ten or more. Confirm the current total when booking.

What is the difference between steam cleaning and chemical wash?

Steam cleaning uses high-pressure steam to clean the coil and blower. Chemical wash adds a coil-cleaning solution followed by a steam flush. The appropriate service depends on the unit's condition.

What is a chemical overhaul?

A chemical overhaul involves taking down and dismantling the indoor unit so its components can be cleaned more thoroughly. Ask the team to confirm the exact scope and price for your model.

Do you serve HDB flats, condos and landed homes?

The company lists HDB flats, condominiums, landed homes and offices among its service locations. Share your postal code to confirm availability.

What information should I provide when booking?

Send the service you are interested in, the number of aircon units, your postal code and a short description or photo of the issue.
