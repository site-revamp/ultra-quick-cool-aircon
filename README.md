# Ultra Quick Cool Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Ultra Quick Cool provides aircon servicing, cleaning, and maintenance for homes and offices in Singapore. The homepage draft helps visitors choose a cleaning method, compare per-unit rates, and request a quote through WhatsApp.

## Main Improvements

1. **Show how unit count affects pricing.** A dedicated price guide lists per-unit rates by quantity for general servicing, two steam-cleaning options, chemical wash, chemical overhaul, and compressor wash. The hero specifies that the S$15 starting rate applies to bookings of 10 or more general servicing units. Confirm the rates and any additional charges before publication.
2. **Show what the cleaning process involves.** A photo section illustrates high-pressure steam cleaning, the use of protective covers, and work on a high-mounted unit. The draft also explains the cooling and drainage checks. Confirm photo provenance and any antibacterial or guarantee claims, and add verified customer reviews with source links.
3. **Simplify service selection and WhatsApp enquiries.** A common-problems guide helps visitors choose between routine and deeper cleaning. Electrical and plumbing appear in their own section. The quote form prepares a WhatsApp message with the selected service, unit count, postal code, and any timing preferences entered in the details field. Confirm the legal entity, UEN, address, operating hours, and service coverage before launch.

## Design Improvements

Consistent service cards and pricing layouts make the cleaning methods and quantity tiers easy to scan. A four-step booking guide shows what customers can expect from their first enquiry through the final checks.
